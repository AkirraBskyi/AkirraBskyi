### About me

I build financial models and research tools, and I test my own results before I trust them.
In my strategy study, none of 180 trend variants survived a multiple-testing check, and I published that result as it was.

- 📊 **Valuation & modelling:** three-statement models, DCF, WACC, sensitivity analysis
- 📈 **Quant research:** backtesting, VaR/CVaR, CAPM, Fama-French, Black-Litterman, options & Greeks
- 🤖 **AI for markets:** Claude-based agents and skills for macro FX research
